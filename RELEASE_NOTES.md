# 2.2.2

- **Two new WoW: Forever secrets:**
  - **Night Watchman's Torch** — an instant quest, An Unfortunate End, from a
    slain Night Watch Guard in Duskwood. The guard has six
    reported spots, each with a map pin.
  - **Scarlet Banner** — collect four Echoes from graves (one inside the
    Scarlet Monastery Graveyard, three outdoors with map pins), activate them
    at the statues in the Library in the right order, then loot the banner.
- **Steps you have already done no longer turn red again.** Some steps
  (talking to an NPC, or an item the chain later uses up) leave nothing the
  addon can check. Secrets that must be done in order, such as Dawn Waker and
  the Scarlet Banner, now count every step before your furthest progress as
  done. Thanks to Steelrat333 for reporting it.
- **Dawn Waker follows the 16 November guide.** The torch steps also tick
  once you have done the torch quest, even if you threw the torch away.
